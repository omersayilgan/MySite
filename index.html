01 About

I'm a control and GNC engineer with hands-on experience across the full loop: controller design in MATLAB/Simulink, code generation for embedded targets, and commissioning on real hardware.

At Roketsan I designed and validated controllers for a missile seeker gimbal, from Simulink models through Embedded Coder to Hardware-in-the-Loop testing. At the Munich climate-tech start-up Phlair I worked on the industrial side: Beckhoff PLC programming in TwinCAT, sensor integration, and the Python and database infrastructure behind test-bench monitoring.

My M.Sc. thesis at TUM covers fault-tolerant control of propulsive spacecraft. I built a modular Python GNC framework for landing trajectory optimisation and actuator-fault analysis of a lunar lander.
